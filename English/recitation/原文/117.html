邻家小男孩每天练习拉小提琴,十分勤奋。(practice)

The boy next door practices the violin diligently every day. 

与其不假思索就仓促执行,不如事先仔细做好任务规划。(better)

It is better to plan ahead carefully than to act hastily and unthinkingly. 

随着春天的到来,这些饱受困苦的鹿再次向北迁徙,将狼群与森林抛在身后(head)

With the arrival of spring, the hard-pressed deer head north once more, leaving the wolves and forests behind. 

这些陆地上的冰偕同海冰,将太阳辐射反射出地表,以此保护我们的星球,防止其出现过热的状况。(together with)

The ice on land, together with sea ice, reflects solar radiation back into space, protecting our planet from overheating.
