1.应该鼓励孩子们多参与户外活动。(encourage)

Children should be encouraged to take part in more outdoor activities. 

2.随着人工智能的发展，许多传统行业正面临着巨大的挑战。(With)

With AI gaining momentum, traditional industries are facing tremendous challenges. 

3.工会每个月推出惠民活动，大家只需要网上预约，就可免费体验。(available)

The union rolls out public welfare activities on a monthly basis, which are available to try for free upon online reservation. 

4.在学校的农耕课程中，学生们全程参与田间劳作，体会到了“粒粒皆辛苦“的含义。(participate)

In the school farming courses, students participate in every aspect of field work, and thus gain an understanding of the meaning behind “Every grain comes with toil”. 

5.随着新学期的开始，学生们需要快速调整心态，以适应更加紧张和繁重的学习任务。(adjust…to)

As the new term begins, students need to promptly adjust their mindset to the more intensive and demanding academic workload.
