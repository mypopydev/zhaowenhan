他就是一个普通人。

1.He is anEveryman.

徒步的好处不胜枚举。

2.The benefits of hiking are numerous.

目前红牛依然是能量饮料的老大。

3.For now, Red Bull remains on top, 

它绝非永久的安身之处。

4.It is no dwelling place for permanent settlement.

初冬的午后，我漫步在这所知名学府的校园，踏着铺满落叶的草坪，享受着书香带来的内心安宁。(wander)

5.On an early winter afternoon, I was wandering around the campus of the prestigious university, treading on the lawn covered with fallen leaves, enjoying the inner peace brought by the scent of books.
