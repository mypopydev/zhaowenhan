1.但到了19世纪末，那个时代已经结束了。

But by the end of the 19th century, that era had come to a close.

2.这座北方冰城面对突如其来的大批游客，一时无所适从。

A large number of sudden tourists’ arrival left the northern icy caught off guard. 

3.汉字并非简单的符号，而是图像的组合，能让读者联想到自然万物。

Instead of simple symbols, Chinese characters are a combination of images, allowing readers to associate with all things in nature. 

4.即使少量摄入也会损害年轻人的大脑，并可能增加心脏问题的风险。

Ingesting even small quantities damages young brains and may raise the risk of heart problems.

5.由于长期坚持阅读经典名著，她不仅拓宽了知识面，还培养了批判性思维能力。

She has not only broadened her knowledge, but also fostered her critical thinking ability as a result of long-term exposure to classic literature. 

6.这座主题乐园中的24口钟均未显示正确时间，游客感到时间停滞，因而沉醉于游览体验中。

All the twenty-four clocks in the theme park don’t show the correct time so that the visitors feel time stand still and are absorbed in the experience.

7.年轻人借助多媒体和直播，打破了销售时空的限制，让农产品走出乡村，进入更大的市场。

Through multimedia and livestreaming, young people have broken the time and space barriers to sales, enabliing the agricultural products from rural areas to reach much larger markets.
